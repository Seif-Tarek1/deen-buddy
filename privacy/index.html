Deen Buddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026 · Contact: support@si-systems.si · Controller: the developer of Deen Buddy, reachable at the address above

The short version

  • Your location never leaves your phone. Prayer times are calculated on the device.
  • Nobody sees your prayer log unless you put them in a Circle and choose to show them.
  • A missed prayer is never shown to anyone, under any setting.
  • When you pause a day, the reason is never shared.
  • You can delete everything, immediately, from inside the app.
  • No ads. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

What this covers

The Deen Buddy apps for Android and iOS, and the backend they talk to.

Information about your religion

Records of your prayers reveal religious belief and practice. Under the UK and EU GDPR this is special-category personal data (Article 9). We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give when you create an account. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account, which erases the data immediately.

If you use the app without an account, this data never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Only on your device, never sent to us

  • Your location, used to calculate prayer times. Coordinates are never transmitted or stored on our servers. You can revoke the permission in your phone's settings, or type a city instead.

Stored on our servers, only if you create an account

  • Account: email address, username, display name and year of birth. The year of birth exists only to apply protections for under-16s.
  • Prayer records: which prayer, which day, its time window, when you logged it, and whether it was combined while travelling. Days you mark as paused are stored with a reason that is visible to you alone.
  • Progress: XP entries, streaks, stages, achievements, and your qada' plan if you make one. The qada' plan is always private.
  • Social: your Circles, friend connections, nudges and du'a you send or receive, shared dhikr contributions, missions, reports you make, and your invite code.
  • Settings: calculation method, privacy choices and display preferences.
  • Device push token: if you allow notifications, so that a Circle-mate's nudge or du'a can reach you. It is deleted when you delete your account.

We do not use advertising identifiers and there is no analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

Using it without an account

The daily prayer loop works with no account. In that mode nothing leaves your phone. Circles need an account, because another person has to be able to find you.

Who can see your prayer data

Nobody, by default. When you join a Circle, its members can see prayers you have completed. Beyond that:

  • Open Book is off unless you turn it on. It lets a Circle see which prayers you have not logged yet, which is what makes nudging possible.
  • Missed prayers are never visible to anyone. No setting reveals them.
  • Paused days appear to others only as "paused", whatever the reason.
  • Log times are shared only if you switch that on.

You can leave a Circle at any time. It takes effect immediately and nobody is told.

Who we share with

Only the services needed to run the app, under data-processing terms:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Expo (EAS): app builds, updates and push-notification relay.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (APNs): delivering notifications to your device.
  • Our email provider: sending sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We will disclose data only if legally compelled, and will tell you unless prohibited.

Where it is stored

In the European Union (Frankfurt). Any transfer outside the UK or EEA relies on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything is removed immediately. Backups roll off within 30 days.

Your rights

Access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. Two are built into the app:

  • Me → Account → Download my data gives you a complete machine-readable export.
  • Me → Account → Delete account erases everything, immediately and irreversibly.

For anything else, email support@si-systems.si. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Under-16 accounts get extra protections automatically: adults cannot find them or send them friend requests, and free-text messages to and from them are disabled, leaving only a curated list of encouragements. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Row-level security on every table, so one user cannot read another's records. Encryption in transit and at rest. Prayer records become fixed once their window closes, and the XP ledger is append-only. No system is perfect; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the regulator within 72 hours.

Changes

We will post changes here, and tell you in the app before any material change takes effect.

Delete your account

How to delete your Deen Buddy account and everything stored with it.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Deen Buddy and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email seifmarzouk40@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Deen Buddy account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything linked to your account: your profile, prayer records, paused days, streaks and XP, Circles memberships, friends, nudges and du'a, missions, stages, your qada' plan, achievements, invite code, settings, consent record and device push tokens. Nothing is kept except encrypted backups, which roll off within 30 days.

Circles you created stay for their other members, without you in them.

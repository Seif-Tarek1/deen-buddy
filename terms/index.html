1. What Deen Buddy is

Deen Buddy helps you keep track of your daily prayers and stay consistent with people you choose. It calculates prayer times on your device and lets you share your progress with a Circle.

XP, levels, streaks and ranks are product features that measure behaviour the app can see. They are not religious rulings and say nothing about the value of anyone's worship. Prayer times are calculated estimates; for anything that matters, follow your local masjid or a qualified scholar.
